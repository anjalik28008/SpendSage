# ECONOMICS — SpendSage

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits at 15-30% off retail pricing. A startup spending $500/month on AI tools might buy $6,000/year of credits through Credex, saving $900-1,800/year.

Estimated deal sizes:
- Small team 5-15 people: $2,500-6,000 in credits, $250-900 gross margin at 12%
- Mid-size team 15-50 people: $6,000-30,000 in credits, $600-4,500 gross margin
- Growth-stage 50-150 people: $30,000-150,000 in credits, $3,000-22,500 gross margin

Blended average deal value: $8,000 in credits purchased per converted customer.
Gross margin at 12%: $960 per customer.
Adjusted LTV with 65% annual renewal: $1,900-2,880 per customer.

## CAC at Each GTM Channel

- HN and Reddit organic: $0 cash, 50-80 audits month 1, 1-2 purchases, near zero CAC
- X direct outreach: $0 cash, 20-30 audits, 0-1 purchases, near zero CAC
- Newsletter placement: $0 editorial, 30-50 audits, 1 purchase, near zero CAC
- Product Hunt launch: $0 cash, 200-400 audits, 3-5 purchases, near zero CAC
- Credex customer list warm email: $0, 100-200 audits, 5-10 purchases, near zero CAC

## Conversion Funnel

1000 visitors
→ 450 audit completions at 45% rate
→ 157 see high-savings CTA at 35% of completions
→ 16 book consultations at 10% of CTA viewers
→ 4-5 credit purchases at 30% of consultations

Revenue per 1000 visitors: 4.5 x $8,000 = $36,000 in credit sales = $4,320 gross margin.

## What Must Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

$1M ARR = $1M gross margin from SpendSage-attributed leads.
At 12% margin: $8.3M in credit sales needed.
At $8,000 average deal: 1,040 new customers over 18 months = 58 per month by month 18.

The math cascade:
- 58 new customers per month
- At 30% consultation-to-purchase: 193 consultations per month
- At 10% CTA-to-consultation: 1,930 high-savings audits per month
- At 35% seeing CTA: 5,514 completions per month
- At 55% completion rate: 10,000 visitors per month needed by month 18

10,000 monthly visitors is achievable with consistent content presence, SEO for AI tool cost comparison keywords, Product Hunt post-launch organic, and Credex customer list as ongoing warm channel.

## Is This Worth Building

Yes clearly. The tool costs near zero ongoing. Vercel free tier handles first 100k users. Development cost is one internship week.

Even at 0.5% visitor-to-purchase rate, 10,000 monthly visitors generates 50 new customers per month x $8,000 deal = $400,000 per month in credit sales = $48,000 per month gross margin. Annualised: $576,000 GM from a free tool that cost one week to build.
