## FAQ

Q: Is this actually free? What is the catch?
A: Completely free to run. We ask for your email after showing results, never before. For teams with significant savings, we flag that Credex sells discounted AI credits. No catch, no spam.

Q: Do you store my company spending data?
A: Your email and company name are never shown in the public shareable link. The public version shows tools and savings numbers only. Data is stored securely in Supabase and never sold or shared.

Q: How do you know the pricing is accurate?
A: Every price is cited to an official vendor pricing page URL in PRICING_DATA.md with the date verified. We update it when vendors change pricing. If you find an error open a GitHub issue and we will fix it within 24 hours.

Q: My company uses enterprise pricing. Will the audit still work?
A: For enterprise plans with custom pricing, enter your actual monthly spend and we use that as the baseline rather than list price.

Q: What is Credex and why are they behind this?
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits from companies that overforecast their usage. SpendSage is a free tool that genuinely helps any team. For teams with large savings opportunities, Credex can often get the same tools at 15-30% less.
